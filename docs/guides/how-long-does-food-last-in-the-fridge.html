How long does food last in the fridge?

It depends on the food, how it was stored and whether it's been opened. Most leftovers keep for three to four days, raw poultry and fish for only a day or two. Here are the typical numbers, with the habits that make food last longer.

First, get the temperature right

Food keeps much longer when the fridge is cold enough. Aim for 4°C (40°F) or below, and keep the freezer at -18°C (0°F). A cheap fridge thermometer is the easiest way to check. A fridge that runs warmer than that shortens the time food stays safe.

Typical fridge times

These are general guidelines for food kept at or below 4°C (40°F), based on US Department of Agriculture advice. Always check the date and storage instructions on the pack, which come first.

FoodIn the fridge
Raw poultry, raw ground meat, raw fish1 to 2 days
Raw steaks, chops and roasts3 to 5 days
Cooked leftovers3 to 4 days
Opened deli meat3 to 5 days
Eggs in their shell3 to 5 weeks
Hard-boiled eggsabout 1 week

The USDA keeps a FoodKeeper tool with storage times for hundreds of foods, and a cold food storage chart. For milk, yogurt, soft cheese, cut fruit and anything else with a date, trust the date and the pack's instructions.

The freezer stops the clock

Food kept at -18°C (0°F) stays safe indefinitely. What changes over time is quality: texture, colour and flavour slowly decline, so freezer food is best used within a few months. Freeze food while it's fresh, not on its last day, wrap it well to avoid freezer burn and label it with the date. Once food has thawed, use it promptly and follow the pack's instructions.

Pantry foods last longest

Dry goods like pasta, rice and flour keep for a long time if they're kept dry and sealed. According to the USDA, high-acid tinned foods such as tomatoes and fruit are best within 12 to 18 months, and low-acid tinned foods such as meat and most vegetables within 2 to 5 years. Throw away any tin that's bulging, leaking or badly dented. Once opened, move the contents into a covered container in the fridge.

Opened vs unopened

Opening a pack changes the clock. Unopened tinned tomatoes last for months in the cupboard; once opened they last a few days in the fridge. The same goes for cooked pasta sauce, jarred food, milk and yogurt. Check the pack for an "after opening" instruction.

When in doubt

Storage times are a guide, not a guarantee. A fridge that's too warm, a long trip home from the shop or a pack that was left out on the counter all shorten how long food lasts. If you don't know how long something has been in the fridge, or you're not sure how it was stored, it's safer to throw it away than to gamble. Pregnant women, older people, young children and anyone with a weakened immune system should be especially careful with chilled foods.

Habits that help

  • Keep raw meat and fish on the bottom shelf so juices can't drip onto food you'll eat uncooked.
  • Cool leftovers quickly and put them in the fridge within two hours (one hour on a hot day).
  • Label leftovers with the day you cooked them.
  • Put new food at the back, and the oldest at the front where you'll see it.
  • Don't rely on smell alone. The bacteria that cause food poisoning don't always change the look or smell of food.
