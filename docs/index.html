Food expiry & kitchen tracker for iPhone

Eat it before it goes off.

Mottainai (もったいない) is the Japanese feeling of regret when something good goes to waste. The app keeps track of what's in your fridge, freezer and pantry and shows what to use first.

Get Mottainai on the App Store

What it does

  • Scan the date. Point the camera at "best before", "use by", EXP, MHD, 賞味期限 or 유통기한 and the date is read on your iPhone.
  • Sensible estimates. Pick a food and get a typical storage time for the fridge, freezer or pantry, and a shorter one once it's opened.
  • Use first. Everything sorted by what needs eating soonest. Swipe right when you've used it, left if it had to go.
  • One morning reminder, only when something needs eating.
  • Shopping list that fills itself as you finish food.
  • Pro: scan a grocery receipt to add a whole shop, waste insights, Home Screen widgets, extra places and recipe ideas made on-device with Apple Intelligence.

No account. No ads. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Help

The camera doesn't find the date. Hold the pack flat, fill the frame with the date and make sure it's in focus. You can also tap the date on screen, choose a photo instead, or set the date by hand.

A date is wrong. Tap the food, then Edit. Estimated dates are general guidance; always check the pack and trust your senses.

Reminders don't arrive. Check Settings › Notifications › Mottainai, and the reminder time in the app's Settings.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Your data. Your kitchen is stored only on your iPhone. Settings › Export kitchen (CSV) saves a copy.
