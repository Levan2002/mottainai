Mottainai: Food Expiry Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short: your kitchen stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The foods you add, their dates, places, notes, your shopping list, history and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Camera images used to read dates and receipts are processed on the device by Apple's Vision framework and are not saved or uploaded. Recipe ideas are generated on the device by Apple Intelligence when it is available.

Purchases

Mottainai Pro is sold through the App Store. To check whether Pro is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase history for this app. This is used only to provide the purchase (app functionality); it is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't collect

No name, email, location, contacts, photos, analytics or advertising identifiers. The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase all data in the app, or delete the app.
